                  Electric Waste Collection
                                         Seungeui Byun, Hong Ki Kim, and Sang Hyun Lee




   Abstract—Smart waste collection uses real-time sensing to
coordinate electric fleets as waste demands, traffic conditions,
and vehicle states change. Vehicle assignment determines each
vehicle’s workload, while routing determines its payload evolution
and traffic exposure, linking them through energy consumption.
This paper develops an adaptive energy-aware fleet coordination
framework that represents assignment and routing within a
unified graphical model. Message-passing implements dynamic-
programming principles on this graph to distribute collection
tasks and derive energy-efficient routes, with routing-energy
metrics coordinating both decisions. The framework directly
updates assignments and routes as operating conditions change,
without retraining or restarting a fleet-wide search. A real-map
digital-twin testbed evaluates this adaptability under varying
traffic, waste demand, and fleet conditions. Numerical results
